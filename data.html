Datasets

Published research datasets, free to download.

Dataset

WHiCH (Western Himalaya Canopy Height) Map 2020, Pakistan

Adeel Ahmad, Srikumar Sastry, Aayush Dhakal, Subash Khanal, Alex Levering, Hammad Gilani and Nathan Jacobs

Zenodo, 2025 · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.18056032

Zenodo Awesome GEE Community Catalog GEE App Bibtex Paper

10 m canopy height map of Pakistan’s western Himalayas for 2020, produced by fusing GEDI LiDAR with Sentinel-2 imagery in a deep learning model.

Dataset

National-Scale Soil Erosion Dataset of Pakistan (2005 and 2015) at 1 km Spatial Resolution

Hammad Gilani, Adeel Ahmad, Isma Younes and Sawaid Abbas

Zenodo, 2024 · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.10694225

Zenodo Awesome GEE Community Catalog GEE App Bibtex Paper

RUSLE-based annual soil loss maps for all of Pakistan for 2005 and 2015, released with our Land Degradation & Development study.

Dataset

Fields of The World (FTW): Global Agricultural Field Boundary Benchmark

Hannah Kerner, Snehal Chaudhari, Aninda Ghosh, Caleb Robinson, Adeel Ahmad, Eddie Choi, Nathan Jacobs, Chris Holmes, Matthias Mohr, Rahul Dodhia, Juan M Lavista Ferres and Jennifer Marcus

Benchmark dataset, 2025 (AAAI-25)

Project Page Code & Data Paper

Over 1.6 million labelled field parcels across 24 countries on four continents for training and testing field boundary segmentation models.
